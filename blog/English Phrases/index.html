A

adapt(oneself)tosthvi. vt. 适应to change one's ideas or behavior in order to deal with a new situation successfully
  • Some animals adapt to new surroundings.（一些动物会适应新的环境。）——唐顿庄园 第三季
  • I guess he had a lot on his mind trying to adapt to the life on the inside.（我猜他在想办法适应牢里的生活。）——肖申克的救赎
  • Look, it's an AI, an artificial intelligence. It can think for itself, adapt itself to new situations.（看，这是一个 AI，人工智能。它本身能够自主思考，使自身适应新的情境。）——星际之门亚特兰蒂斯 第二季
  • When you go to a foreign country, you must adapt yourself to new manners and customs.（你去外国时，必须使自己适应新的风俗习惯。）——刘毅词汇5000
addition(to sth)n1. 增加物；添加物something that you add to sth else
  • There is about to be a new addition to the family.（家里即将迎来新成员。）——美丽中国
  • I thought it would be a nice addition to your collection.（我觉得它会让你收藏锦上添花。）——绝望的主妇 第五季
  • One of the latest additions to the tree of life is a new type of leopard frog.（生命之树最新添加入一种新的豹蛙。）——科学美国人-60秒科学
n2. 扩建建筑，增建部分an extension that is added to a building to improve it
  • The new addition you built, it's just amazing.（你新扩建的那部分，真漂亮。）——人类清除计划
  • Are you putting an addition on the house?（你要扩建房子吗？）——曼哈顿计划 第一季
  • It's some of the locals helping us build an addition to the school.（当地的居民们正在帮助我们扩建学校。）——海岸罪案调查处 第九季
in addition(to sb/sth)IDM. 此外；除…以外used when you want to introduce an additional point or relevant fact
  • Please submit your supporting documents. In addition, you will be required to pay an application fee.（请提交你的证明文件。此外，你将需要支付申请费。）——你问我答
  • I can sing, too, you know, in addition to the ice skating.（除了滑冰，我还会唱歌。）——女子监狱 第一季
  • In addition to being a boss, I am also a waitress.（我除了是老板之外，还是一名服务员。）——破产姐妹 第二季
the addition ofsthn. 增添，添加the act of adding one thing to another
  • The addition of a child would disrupt even the happiest of couples.（有了孩子，就算最幸福的伴侣也会受到影响。）——犯罪心理 第十季
  • The British Empire expanded rapidly with the addition of Borneo, Burma and New Guinea.（随着婆罗洲、缅甸和新几内亚的加入，大英帝国迅速扩张。）——黑猫系列读物
  • However, the addition of water to the land or soil may cause problems in some areas.（然而在有的地区，田地里水流的添加会造成问题。）——VOA慢速-农业报道
advance(on sth)n1. 预付款money which is paid to sb before the usual time of payment
  • I need an advance on my pay.（我需要提前预支些钱。）——亲密治疗
  • I had to give Howie an advance on his allowance.（我只好让华仔预支他的零用钱。）——生活大爆炸 第七季
n2. （价格、价值的）上涨，上升a rise in the price, value, of sth
  • Any advance on 22'000? Anyone?（还有比22000更高的出价吗?还有吗?）——碟中谍5
  • Chumhum has agreed to an advance on our fees. It was Cary's case.（Chumhum网已经同意提高代理费。这是Cary的案子。）——傲骨贤妻 第六季
in advance(of sth)IDM. 超前于某人/某物more developed or modern than sb or sth else
  • Already far in advance of Egypt, their legal position has not changed since the revolution.（在这点上已经比埃及强很多，在革命之后，到现在为止，她们的法律地位也没有丝毫的改变。）——综合-经济学人
sbof advanced yearsIDM. 高龄的，年事已高的used to say 'old' in a polite way
  • Rockers of advanced years are also good for busting stereotypes about older workers.（年长的摇滚歌手也有助于打破关于老年工人的刻板印象。）——综合-经济学人
  • He was a man of advanced years.（他是个年长的男人。）——精选例句
sb'sadvanced ageIDM. 高龄的，年事已高的used to say 'old' in a polite way
  • But you may not remember because of your advanced age.（但你可能记不起来了，因为你年纪大了。）——生活大爆炸 第七季
  • They engage in many practices thought to confer good health and survival to an advanced age.（他们进行各种锻炼，认为这些有助于健康和长寿。）——TED Talks
a advantage of/to (doing) sthn. 优点a good or desirable quality or feature that makes sth better or more useful
  • I thought that one of the advantages of marrying an older guy was that I was going to be able to relax.（我认为嫁给一个年纪大的人的一个好处就是我可以过得轻松点。）——摩登家庭 第一季
  • I like your new office. It has the advantage of being remote.（我喜欢你的新办公室。它有一个好处是远离闹市。）——白宫风云 第五季
  • There is a big advantage to traveling during the low season, though.（不过，在淡季旅行确实有很多好处。）——ESL Podcast
a advantage oversbn. 有利条件，优势something that puts you in a better position than other people
  • What gives you a distinct advantage over all the other capable people here?（相比其他有能力的人，你有什么明显的优势？）——亿万 第二季
  • You were always the better engineer. But I do have one advantage over you.（你当工程师一直比我高明，但我确实有一项优势。）——疑犯追踪 第二季
be/work toone'sadvantageIDM. 对…有利to give you an advantage; (to be) better for you
  • It'll definitely be to our advantage to have someone who can go in and out freely.（有人可以自由进出对我们来说绝对是优势。）——死亡笔记 英文配音版
  • I used to swim in college — the one place being tall actually worked to my advantage.（我上大学的时候经常游泳，在大学个子高对我来说很有利。）——英美剧集
take advantage ofsthIDM. 利用if you take advantage of sth, you make good use of it while you can
  • Glad to see you're taking advantage of the opportunities offered.（很高兴看到你利用了提供给你的机会。）——英美剧集
  • Just taking advantage of the local resources.（我只是在利用本地资源。）——神秘博士 第七季
  • Harry took advantage of the temporary silence to eat more breakfast.（哈利利用这短暂的沉默，加紧吃他的早餐。）——哈利波特与混血王子
take advantage ofsbIDM. 占…的便宜；欺骗；捉弄if sb takes advantage of you, they treat you unfairly for their own benefit
  • Frank's just too soft hearted, and people take advantage of him.（弗兰克就是心太软了，人们利用他这一点来占他的便宜。）——《飘》
  • He thinks he can take advantage of you because you're a woman.（他觉得你是女人所以就可以占你的便宜。）——我为喜剧狂 第三季
  • I went down to the free clinic and I took full advantage of the "free" part for you.（我去了免费诊所，并为你大大占了它"免费"的便宜。）——衰女翻身 第一季
take the advantage ofsthn. 有利条件，优势something that puts you in a better position than other people
  • So Angel did not have the advantage of a university education.（所以安吉尔没有大学教育的有利条件。）——德伯家的苔丝
  • As I was saying, Maureen, I was really at an advantage having seen the Broadway production.（正如我说的，莫琳，我确实占了优势，因为我已经看过百老汇的演出。）——摩登家庭 第四季
to good/best advantageIDM. 最有利地；最有效地；使优点突出in a way that shows the best features of sth
  • It showed to advantage the good shape of his figure.（这套衣服凸显了他的好身材。）——还乡
  • There his faculties stagnate — they cannot develop or appear to advantage.（在这里他的才能会停滞不前，难以提升或有效施展出来。）——简 · 爱（原著）
use/turnsthtoone'sadvantageIDM. 最有利地；最有效地；使优点突出to use sth in order to benefit from it, especially when it might be expected to harm or damage you
  • I'm going to use the rules of the Death Note to my advantage.（我打算利用死亡笔记的规则。）——死亡笔记 英文配音版
  • I saw an opportunity to turn things to my advantage, and I did.（我看到有机会可以把事情转变成对我有利的条件，我就利用了这个机会。）——闪电侠 第一季
an alternative tosthn. 可供选择的事物；可供替代的事物sth that can be chosen instead of sth else
  • Is there no alternative to retreat then?（除了撤退，没有别的路可以选了吗?）——罗马 第一季
  • The only alternative to being taken prisoner was to die fighting.（不想当俘虏，唯一的选择就是誓死而战。）——淘金式巧攻大学英语词汇
anxietyabout/oversthn. 忧虑，焦虑，不安anxiety is a feeling of nervousness or worry
  • I don't feel any anxiety about safety right now.（我现在一点也不担心安全问题。）——NPR 听力
  • Have you had any anxiety about coming back to work?（你回来上班的时候有没有担心过？）——海军罪案调查处
  • Some conceal anxiety about growing older.（有些人掩饰自己对年华逝去的恐惧。）——绝望的主妇
anxiety forfor/to dosthn. 渴望，热望a strong feeling of wanting to do sth very much
  • The teacher praised him for his anxiety for knowledge.（老师表扬了他对知识的渴望。）——雅思词汇例句
  • More thoughtfulness and less anxiety to please, than when they last met, were plainly expressed.（比上次见面时更深思熟虑，更少焦虑去取悦，这些都明显地表达了出来。）——傲慢与偏见
anxious aboutsb/sthadj. 焦虑的；忧虑的；不安的if you are anxious, you are nervous or worried about sth
  • My family's anxious about this trip.（我家人担心这次旅行。）——征服珠峰
  • The result: we can become so anxious about making the wrong choice, we end up making no choice at all.（结果：我们可能会因为害怕做出错误的选择，而最终选择不做任何选择。）——如何找到满意的工作
anxious forfor/to do/thatsthadj. 渴望；非常希望wanting sth very much
  • I've been so anxious for your arrival.（我在这恭候多时了。）——勇敢者游戏：决战丛林
  • Oh, good. Here you are. Mom and dad are anxious for an update. Dr. Yang?（哦，太好了。你来了。妈妈和爸爸渴望得到更新。Yang医生？）——实习医生格蕾
  • Both Mr. Pilkington and Mr. Frederick were anxious to buy it.（皮尔金顿先生和弗雷德里克先生都非常渴望买到它。）——动物农场
  • I am anxious to get back to my aunt and uncle, for I am sure they will worry about me.（我渴望回家看看我的阿姨和叔叔，因为我知道他们会担心我。）——绿野仙踪
  • You see...I'm anxious to make Lady Mary happy.（你可以看到我渴望让她高兴。）——唐顿庄园
  • He, of course, was very anxious that no one should disobey her, or make her angry.（他当然非常担心没有人会听从她，或者让她不高兴。）——呼啸山庄
atmosphere ofsthn. 气氛，氛围the mood or feeling of a place or situation
  • I spent a month at Moor House, in an atmosphere of warm friendship.（我在摩尔大厦度过了一个月，那里充满了温暖的友谊。）——简·爱
  • Chandler: What I do do...is manage to create an atmosphere of support for the people working with me.（我做的就是创造一个支持性的氛围，让和我一起工作的人感到舒适。）——老友记
audience withsbn. （与要人的）正式会见；拜见；谒见a formal meeting that you have with an important person
  • We're seeking an audience with Robert Burnett, and he's the CEO.（我们正在寻求与罗伯特·伯内特的会见，他是首席执行官。）——福尔摩斯：演绎法
  • I got an audience with Congressman Michael Barnes.（我见到了迈克尔·巴恩斯议员。）——律政俏师太
  • I want you to get me a private audience with Prince Eric.（我想让你安排一次私人会见，与埃里克王子见面。）——Grimm 格林
  • Our request this audience, Father, to discuss a matter of great importance.（我们请求这次会见，父亲，是为了讨论一个重要的问题。）——梅林传奇
authority onsthn. （某一方面的）权威人士，专家，泰斗someone who knows a lot about a particular subject
  • Doctor Andrew Ingersoll is an authority on the planet.（Andrew Ingersoll博士是行星方面的专家。）——宇宙
  • A leading authority on snakes, Bryan recently turned his attention to the hunting strategy of lizards.（作为蛇类方面的权威，Bryan最近将注意力转向了蜥蜴的狩猎策略。）——自然世界：死亡之吻——科莫多巨蜥
authority to dosthn. 职权the power or right to do sth
  • You don't have the authority to fire him.（你没有权力开除他。）——明星伙伴
  • You no longer have the authority to carry a weapon.（你无权携带武器。）——芝加哥警署 Chicago P.D.
  • The Stafford Act gives me full authority to define an emergency.（《Stafford法案》赋予我定义紧急情况的全部权力。）——纸牌屋
havesthon good authorityIDM. 有可靠的消息来源to be able to believe sth because you trust the person who told you about it
  • I have it on good authority that you're right.（我有很确凿的证据来证明你是对的。）——广告狂人
  • I have it on good authority that your life's in danger.（我有可靠消息，你现在有生命危险。）——疑犯追踪

B

ban onsthn. 禁止，禁令，禁…令an official ruling that sth must not be done, shown, or used
  • At that time there was a national ban on alcohol.（在当时，美国是有禁酒令的。）——VOA慢速英语——建国史话
  • Last year, the DEA issued an emergency ban on it.（去年，缉毒局发布了一项紧急禁令。）——识骨寻踪
  • They asked him to lift a ban on new settlers from the United States.（他们要求他取消对新移民来自美国的限制。）——VOA慢速英语——建国史话
  • And Beijing now says it will enforce a total ban by the end of the year.（北京表示将在今日年底前全面禁止（象牙）交易。）——CRI 听力
bansbfrom (doing)sthvt. 禁止（某人）做某事to officially prevent sb from doing sth
  • Luke, you are now banned from the computer.（Luke，从今以后你不准你用电脑。）——摩登家庭
  • She'll ban you from the library if you're not careful. Why did you have to bring that stupid book?（你要是不小心点儿，她会禁止你进图书馆的。你干吗非得带那本愚蠢的书？）——哈利波特与混血王子
  • The Taliban had banned girls from attending school.（塔利班禁止女孩上学。）——慢速英语
  • They pointed to a 1975 district law that banned most people from having handguns.（他们指出，1975年的地区法律禁止大多数人拥有手枪。）——美国最高法院的历史
basis insthn. 基础，要素the most important facts, ideas, etc. form which sth can be developed
  • Stories that had no basis in truth?（完全虚构的故事吗？）——傲骨贤妻
  • Please, sir, don't legends always have a basis in fact?（拜托了，先生，传说都是有一定的事实基础的，不是吗？）——哈利波特密室
the/abasis forsthn. 基础，要素the most important facts, ideas, etc. form which sth can be developed
  • This should form the basis for our marketing plan.（这会构成我们市场营销计划的基础。）——BEC中级商务英语真题2
  • The polymer was a chemical product that formed the basis for Kevlar.（这种聚合物是制造出凯夫拉的基础物质）——VOA慢速——科学新闻
  • These points served as a basis for debate.（这些关键点成为了讨论国家行政的基础。）——VOA慢速英语——建国史话
the/abasis ofsthn. 基础，要素the most important facts, ideas, etc. form which sth can be developed
  • Openness is the basis of free society.（公正公开是自由社会的基础。）——白宫风云
  • It is the basis of all our faith and all our hope.（它是我们所有信念和希望的基础。）——BBC 听力
  • As we made a transition to the idea that marriage should be on the basis of love, it scared people.（当我们转变到婚姻应该建立在爱的基础上的观念时，它吓坏了人们。）——流行文化
bump against/onsthvi. vt. 猛碰，撞to hit or knock against sth
  • I don't want to bump her head against the door, you know, pinch her with the seat belt.（我不想又把她头撞到门上了，或者说，绑安全带的时候夹到她。）——摩登家庭 第一季
  • Remember the time you fell off your bike or bumped your head on a sharp corner?（还记得你从自行车上摔下或一头撞到尖角之时吗？）——TED-Ed
bump intosb/sthvi. vt. 猛碰，撞to hit or knock against sth
  • She stepped backward and bumped into someone.（她踉跄后退，结果撞到了别人。）——权力的游戏 有声书
  • If I move slowly, I won't bump into things.（如果我走得慢点，就不会撞到东西了。）——小猪佩奇 第一季

interact withsbvi. 相互交流，相互沟通when people interact with each other or interact, they communicate as they work or spend time together
  • Is he the only person you interact with?（他是唯一和你有交流的人吗？）——摩登家庭 第一季
  • Number 6: he never interacts with youon social media.（第六点，他从来不在社交媒体上和你互动。）——生活百科

interact withsthvi. 相互作用，相互影响to act on or have an effect on each other
  • Still, it is just a story of how electrons interact with light.（然而，光与电子的相互作用仅仅是一个故事。）——VOA 慢速-自然探索
  • It's all about how one substance interacts with another to give us something new.（其本质在于两种物质发生化学反应从而生成新物质。）——化学公开课

interaction with/betweensbn. 交流，沟通the activity of talking to other people, and the way that people react to each other
  • He needs social interaction with his peers.（他需要和同龄人进行社交。）——无耻家庭 美版 第六季
